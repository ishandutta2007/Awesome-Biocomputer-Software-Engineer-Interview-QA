# Resources: Further Reading & Tools for Biocomputer Software Engineers

## Landmark Papers
- Bhaskaran et al. (2023+) — Johns Hopkins Organoid Intelligence initiative papers (search current literature)
- Obaid et al. and related MEA/neural interface hardware-software integration literature
- NWB format specification and adoption papers (Teeters et al.)
- Shenoy, Sahani & Churchland (2013) — "Cortical control of arm movements: a dynamical systems perspective" (Annual Review of Neuroscience) — population coding/neural manifold concepts
- Lewicki (1998) — "A review of methods for spike sorting" (Network: Computation in Neural Systems) — foundational spike sorting review

## Textbooks & References
- **"Principles of Neural Science"** (Kandel et al.) — foundational neuroscience textbook
- **"Analysis of Neural Data"** (Kass, Eden & Brown) — statistical neuroscience methods
- **Real-time Linux/RTOS documentation** (kernel.org PREEMPT_RT resources)

## Organizations
- **Organoid Intelligence consortium** (Johns Hopkins) — leading OI research coordination
- **IEEE Brain Initiative** — neural engineering standards and community
- **OpenNeuro / NWB ecosystem** — open data and standardized formats
